<h1>Team G Contract</h1>

<h3>Communication:</h3>

- Absence, illness, and conflict will be communicated within 24 hours when possible

- Replies to Teams messages will be made within 24 hours

-	Work will be completed by scheduled internal deadlines within sprints

<h3>Meetings:</h3>

-	Meetings will take place after class on Mondays, for ~30 minutes

-	Brief standup meetings may also occur on Fridays after class, for ~5 minutes

-	Members will be punctual and focused during meetings

<h3>Responsibilities:</h3>

-	All tasks will be completed 1-2 days before sprint deadline

-	Group members will complete their assigned tasks, with a lower general limit of 3 hours a week spent on assignments

-	Task assignments will be roughly balanced between members, excepting arrangements made due to absence, illness, tests, other work, or the like

-	There are two roles, Project Manager and Lead Engineer, as outlined by the course

<h3>Conflict Resolution:</h3>
In case of illness or unavoidable and communicated conflicts, the team will try to rebalance the work so that all tasks are completed by the end of the sprint.<br> 
<br>
Absent such communication, the following will apply when work is not completed by scheduled internal deadline:

-	Day 1: PM will contact the group member and discuss the work

-	Day 2: Reminder will be made on Teams about work

-	Day 3: Consider professor involvement if needed
